### Week 2 Objectives:

* Become familiar with the basic purposes of Amazon CloudFront and Amazon S3.
* Learn introductory concepts such as S3 buckets and objects, and content delivery through CloudFront.
* Recognize how CloudFront can deliver content stored in an S3 bucket at a high level.

### Week 2 Learning Outcomes:

* Gained an introductory understanding of Amazon S3 as a service for storing objects in buckets.
* Learned at a high level that Amazon CloudFront helps deliver content to users.
* Recognized that an S3 bucket can serve as an origin for content delivered through CloudFront.
* Became familiar with the basic idea of caching and its role in content delivery.
* Sketched a simple content delivery flow using S3 and CloudFront without configuring advanced settings.

These topics were studied at a basic, introductory level; further practice is needed to configure and use the services independently.
